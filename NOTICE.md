# Notice

A demo website by O2 Design Studio for Louvre Fleuriste, published with the shop's approval (2026-09-28). It has no
connection to the Musée du Louvre. Ordering on the demo is a demonstration: orders stay in the visitor's browser
and never reach the shop.

- **Photographs** belong to Louvre Fleuriste. Taken from the shop's public Instagram (@louvrefleuriste), its
  other social pages and its Google Maps listing, on 2026-09-27/28. The shop approved their use on the demo site
  (2026-09-28). They are not in this repository's code (`main`); the built demo on the `gh-pages` branch carries
  web-sized copies. Do not reuse them elsewhere without the shop's permission.
- **Captions and prices** quoted are the shop's own, from its Instagram profile and posts.
- **Booking rules** (lead time, capacity, time windows, closures, budget tiers) are invented samples and are
  marked on screen as such.
- The site is `noindex` (a demo, not the shop's own address). Locally its canonical points at a `.example`
  domain; on GitHub Pages at the demo's own address.
